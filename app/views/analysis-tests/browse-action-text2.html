Ebor Academy Trust said:

We will improve our use of workforce data to inform decision-making by:

  • Tracking gender representation across roles, grades and leadership positions
  • Monitoring recruitment, retention, progression and turnover data
  • Reviewing all people-related policies to ensure they are equitable and inclusive in practice

Edinburgh Airport Limited said:

This year we have started using different jobs boards to advertise our vacancies - Diversity Jobs (LGBT, Disability, BME and Neurodiversity). This allows us to expand our reach and actively target groups of people we wish to attract. We are trialling this on a 12 month period and will monitor via our ATS to assess how many candidates select this as their source of application, and monitor the candidates progress through the recruitment process. We also actively track applications of women to roles and have done targeted outreach events for teams which are underrepresented.

Planned

Fanco Ltd. said:

FANCO LTD is committed to attracting a diverse workforce and providing equal access to employment and career opportunities. We will broaden how and where vacancies are advertised, use inclusive recruitment materials and encourage applications from groups that may be under-represented within particular roles or levels of the organisation. Appointments will continue to be based on skills, experience and suitability for the role. We will monitor applications, shortlisting and appointments by gender to identify trends, assess the effectiveness of our approach and inform future recruitment activity.

Intellectual Property Office said:

We are strengthening our approach to attracting diverse candidates by broadening outreach, reviewing recruitment messaging and working with networks and partners to increase visibility of opportunities. Our focus is on reducing barriers to entry and ensuring that people from underrepresented groups feel confident to apply. This includes considering how roles are presented and ensuring that our employer offer reflects inclusivity, flexibility and support. This work supports longer-term improvements in representation across grades and contributes to addressing gender pay disparities.

Isle of Wight College said:

The College is committed to attracting talent that reflects the communities we serve. Through inclusive recruitment practices, community engagement and accessible advertising, we will continue to encourage applications from a diverse range of local candidates.

Building on the success of our existing approach, we will promote opportunities widely, remove unnecessary barriers to entry where possible, and showcase the College as an inclusive employer, helping to attract, develop and retain talent from across our community.

We are broadening our presence across social channels as another opportunity to reach diverse talent. We will track the impact of diversity through the use of social channels throughout 2026.

Mott Macdonald Limited said:

We take an evidence-led approach to attracting diverse candidates by analysing workforce data across recruitment, progression and representation to identify gaps. This informs targeted attraction strategies. We partner with professional networks, charities and specialist organisations, including STEM Returners, Flexa and myGwork, to reach underrepresented groups and promote inclusive opportunities. We also engage industry and community partners to broaden our reach beyond traditional channels. Internally, we support inclusive hiring through structured, fair recruitment processes and encourage employees to share opportunities within their networks. All candidates are assessed on merit, with clear criteria in place to minimise bias and ensure equitable outcomes.

Woodgate and Clark Limited said:

We identified that the diversity of our applicant pool may be influenced by where and how we advertise roles. Preliminary review highlighted that we have not yet formally assessed the diversity reach or inclusion commitments of all job boards and advertising channels we use. To address this, we will conduct a review of our recruitment advertising platforms to assess audience reach, diversity credentials and alignment with our inclusion aims, and will take action to broaden advertising where appropriate to improve access for candidates from different backgrounds.
