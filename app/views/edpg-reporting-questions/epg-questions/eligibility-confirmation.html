Check what you are eligible to report

You can see what you are eligible to report on based on the information you provided.

Headline comparison

For the headline comparison between the white (including white other) ethnic group and all other ethnic groups combined you are eligible to report for:

  • Ethnicity pay gap, percentage who recieve bonus and bonus pay gap
  • Pay quarters

Further analysis

For the ethnicity pay gap, percentage of employees who recieve bonus pay and bonus pay gap reports you are eligible to report for the below groups:

  • White (including white other)
  • Asian or Asian British
  • Black, Black British, Caribbean or African
  • Mixed or multiple ethnic groups
  • Other ethnic groups

For the pay quarters report you are eligible to report for the below groups:

  • White (including white other)
  • Asian or Asian British
  • Mixed or multiple ethnic groups
  • Other ethnic groups

For the pay quarters report you are not eligible to report for the below groups:

  • Black, Black British, Caribbean or African
