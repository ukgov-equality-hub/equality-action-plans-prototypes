Case Studies

Case studies are real world examples of how employers have implemented actions to address their gender pay gap.

These case studies are shared by employers to demonstrate best practice, show what employers can achieve and help other employers to understand what actions their peers are taking.

You can also see a full list of suggested actions for employers to address their gender pay gap.

Case studies are grouped under actions designed to address employers' Gender Pay Gap or to address Menopause

Menopause

TVP: Maintaining workplace dignity for a Crime Scene Investigator (CSI) who needed reasonable adjustments for menopause symptoms.

The CSI accessed tailored reasonable adjustments to manage menopause symptoms. This enabled her to remain in the job she loved and continue performing effectively.

Employees: More than 20,000

Sector: Public Sector

Bain: Taking action to help with menopause

Bain & Company UK introduced a standalone menopause policy and comprehensive practical support, creating an environment where employees can seek help with confidence.

Employees: More than 20,000

Sector: Consultancy

Middlesex University: Raising awareness of menopause symptoms and reducing workplace taboo

Middlesex University ran a poster campaign and peer support sessions, resulting in more people of all genders being open to workplace conversations about menopause.

Employees: 5,000-19,999

Sector: Education

Cambridgeshire County Council: How Cambridgeshire became a ‘Menopause Friendly’ employer

Cambridgeshire County Council developed and enhanced their support for colleagues experiencing menopause, culminating in recognition as a Menopause Friendly employer.

Employees: 500-999

Sector: Local authority

L&Q: Using training, specialist care and ‘champions’ to improve menopause support

L&Q has created meaningful cultural change around menopause awareness and support. They have done this through safe spaces for talking, specialist GP advice, and menopause ‘champions’.

Employees: 1,000-4,999

Sector: Housing

University of Greenwich: How University of Greenwich embedded menopause awareness and support for staff

The University of Greenwich (UoG) created a culture where conversations about menopause were normalised by providing menopause awareness and support for staff.

Opportunity Green helped to create a supportive working environment for employees experiencing menopausal symptoms, problematic periods or menstrual ill health.
