Barclays: How Barclays enhanced its paternity leave to help working families

At a glance
Employer name Barclays
Employer sector Finance
Number of employees >20,000
Action category Gender Pay Gap
Summary of action and implementation Barclays enhanced its UK paternity leave from 2 to 16 paid weeks to better support working families, improve talent attraction, and foster an inclusive culture. Prompted by low shared parental leave take-up and feedback from unions and colleague networks, the update was announced in November 2025 alongside new guidance and systems. Employee feedback has been overwhelmingly positive, with colleagues praising the organisation for supporting them through major life events.

Background Information

Our people policies help us to:

  • recruit the best people
  • provide equal opportunities
  • create an inclusive culture

This is in line with our purpose, values and mindset, and in support of our long-term success.

The policies are regularly reviewed and updated to ensure they are aligned with our broader people strategy.

Before these changes, we provided UK colleagues with 2 weeks’ paid paternity leave and 4 weeks’ paid shared parental leave. Low take-up of our shared parental leave provision supported enhancing our paid paternity leave provision.

We engage with colleagues through various channels including our unions and colleague communities, such as the working families resource group. Colleagues have told us through these channels that increasing our paternity leave provision could better support colleagues with balancing their home and work lives.

What they wanted to achieve

This significant update was designed to:

  • give new parents more time with their families
  • support colleagues in balancing work and personal life
  • continue to foster an inclusive workplace culture

We will measure the effectiveness of this change through speaking with colleagues. This helps us understand what is working well across the organisation and where we can improve, including through surveys and through our long-standing partnership with Unite.

How they did it

We worked collaboratively on the proposition, taking on board feedback from Unite and our colleague communities. We brought together specialists from across HR, including our inclusion and opportunity team.

In November 2025, we announced the significant enhancement to our paternity leave policy in the UK.

We published guidance for colleagues and managers, and we updated our systems to enable colleagues to book the enhanced leave.

Tristram Roberts, Group HR Director, said:

"This is a major investment in our people and demonstrates Barclays’ commitment to attracting and retaining the best talent, while continuing to build an inclusive environment. Ultimately, it’s about supporting families.” 

This enhancement is part of the comprehensive package of benefits we provide to colleagues.

The outcome

Feedback from colleagues has been enthusiastic. They recognise they have been listened to and that this supports Barclays as a great and supportive place to work:

"Really pleased to see this change in policy. It shows Barclays at its best – supporting colleagues through the life events that we navigate across our careers."

"As a mum who has just returned from maternity leave last week, this news makes me SO happy! I’d have loved for my husband to be off longer with us rather than the standard 2 weeks. He doesn't work for Barclays so this won't impact us as a family if we have another baby, but I am so pleased for the families it will – just another great reason to work for Barclays!"

It is too early to review how many colleagues are making use of the new provision, but the response from colleagues has been very positive.

Advice for other organisations

Further support is available from our dedicated and passionate colleague communities. These include our UK Working Families Resource Group, a community for colleagues to learn more about how to balance working and personal responsibilities and maintain wellbeing.

We have also written about these changes on our website
