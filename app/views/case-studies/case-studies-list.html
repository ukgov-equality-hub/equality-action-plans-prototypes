Case Studies

Case studies are real world examples of how employers have implemented actions to address their gender pay gap.

These case studies are shared by employers to demonstrate best practice, show what employers can achieve and help other employers to understand what actions their peers are taking.

You can also see a full list of suggested actions for employers to addres their gender pay gap.

Case studies are grouped under the same categories as the list of suggested actions:

  • Menopause
  • Recruiting Staff
  • Developing and promoting staff
  • Building diversity in your organisation
  • Increasing transparency
