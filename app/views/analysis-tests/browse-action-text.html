Supporting texts: Make job descriptions inclusive

Guidance: Make job descriptions inclusive

Acorn Mobility Services Ltd. said:

Acorn uses inclusive language in job descriptions and lists only essential requirements, promoting equal opportunities.

This removes unnecessary barriers that may discourage underrepresented groups from applying. By focusing on the skills genuinely needed for the role, we aim to attract a wider, more diverse talent pool.

Inclusive wording also signals that we value fairness and belonging, strengthening our employer brand and helping us hire the best person for the job.

Ebor Academy Trust said:

We will strengthen inclusive recruitment practices across teaching and support roles by:

  • Ensuring all job advertisements use inclusive, gender-neutral language
  • Promoting roles in a way that supports diverse applicants, including underrepresented groups
  • Monitoring diversity data at each stage of the recruitment process (application, shortlisting and appointment)

New or in progress

Edinburgh Airport Limited said:

We continually evolve our job descriptions to reflect changing priorities. All job descriptions follow a standard template, which includes a DEI statement. They will always include the salary or range, and only ask for qualifications which are absolutely necessary. This is to make roles accessible to as wide a pool as possible. We also analyse the language to ensure it is gender neutral or feminine coded, as research shows masculine coded language deters females from applying.

Fanco Ltd. said:

FANCO LTD is committed to ensuring our recruitment processes provide equal opportunities for all candidates. We are reviewing and improving job descriptions to use inclusive, gender-neutral language and ensure requirements are limited to those genuinely necessary for each role. We will clearly communicate our commitment to equality and encourage applications from suitably qualified candidates from all backgrounds. We will monitor recruitment outcomes, including applications, shortlisting and appointments by gender, to identify potential barriers and support continuous improvement in our recruitment practices.
