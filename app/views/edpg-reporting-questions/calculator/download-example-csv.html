Download our example CSV file, then replace the data about our fictitious employees with the data about your own employees

The CSV file contains 5 columns:

  • Gender

    The gender of the employee.
    This should be either Male or Female

  • Ethnicity

    The high-level ethnicity of the employee.
    This should be Asian, Black, Mixed, Other or White.
    If the employee has not declared their ethnicity, leave this blank.

  • Disability

    This should be Yes (for employees with a disability) or No (for employees with no disablity).
    If the employee has not declared their disability status, leave this blank.

  • HourlyPay

    The hourly pay of the employee (in £ per hour). Do not include a £ sign or commas in the number. For example, if an employee receives £22.75 per hour, enter 22.75

  • BonusPayInTheLastYear

    The bonus pay the employee received in the previous year (in £). Do not include a £ sign or commas in the number. For example, if the employee received a bonus of £1,300, enter 1300
