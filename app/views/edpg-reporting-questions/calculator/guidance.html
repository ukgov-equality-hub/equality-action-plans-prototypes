How to use our calculator

  • Download our example CSV file

    We have created an example CSV file.
    The example CSV file contains the details of 5 fictitious employees, to help you understand the format of the data we require (more details on the next page).

  • Fill in details about your employees

    Replace the data about our fictitious employees with the data about your own employees.

  • Upload the CSV to our service

    The CSV file with the details of your employees will be processed and held securely and will never be published.

  • We will check the CSV file

    We will check that the CSV file is in the correct format and ask you to verify some details about your employees, to ensure you've filled it in correctly.

  • We will do all the calculations for you

    Once you are happy with the details of your employees, we will do all the calculations for you and provide you with a report of your pay gaps.

  • You can review and edit the report before you submit

    You will be returned to the manual data entry journey, with all the pay gap data pre-filled, so you can review and edit the report before you submit it.
