Ethnicity pay gap reporting will require employers to report a binary comparison at minimum and also aggregate to 5 ethnic groups where possible.

The minimum comparison is between the white (including white other) ethnic group and all other ethnic groups combined.

The further analysis comparisons are between the white (including white other) ethnic group and each of the other ethnic groups in the GSS harmonised stardard, where eligible.

Ethnicity data calculation will be in line with the GSS harmonised standards, and aggregation in line with ONS guidance.

The 5 ethnic groups for ethnicity pay gap reporting are:

  • White (including white other)
  • Asian or Asian British
  • Mixed or multiple ethnic groups
  • Other ethnic groups

To be eligible to report on your ethnicity pay gap you must meet the minimum criteria for number of employees in each ethnic group (10). This is to protect the identity of individual employees in each group.
