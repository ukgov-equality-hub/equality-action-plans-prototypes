Ethnicity pay gap reporting will require employers to report a binary comparison at minimum and also aggregate to 5 ethnic groups where possible.

The minimum headline comparison is between the white (including white other) ethnic group and all other ethnic groups combined.

To be eligible to report on your ethnicity pay gap you must meet the minimum criteria for number of employees in each ethnic group (10). This is to protect the identity of individual employees in each group.
